About KILAMSA Schools

Mlimba - Morogoro | Since 2010

Our History

KILAMSA Schools started with a vision to provide quality, affordable education from Pre-Primary to Secondary (Form Six) in Mlimba, Morogoro. We combine NECTA curriculum with modern digital learning.

Our Mission

To nurture disciplined, excellent and God-fearing students with academic excellence and integrity.

Our Vision

To be the leading school in Morogoro region for academic results and character formation.

Core Values

  • Excellence - High academic standards
  • Discipline - Respect and order
  • Integrity - Honesty in all we do
  • Faith - Spiritual growth
